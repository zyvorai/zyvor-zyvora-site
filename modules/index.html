Modules

Everything a day on the ward needs.

Twelve modules share one patient record, one sign-in and one audit trail.

Dashboard

Today's timeline, the waiting room, trends and ward bed maps, tuned to the signed-in role.

Patients

Registry, allergies and history, with restricted records that open only through break-glass.

Appointments

Scheduling with no double-booking enforced in the database, check-in and a waiting room.

Encounters

SOAP notes, orders and prescribing, signing and amendments.

Emergency

A tracking board with triage decision support (a trained classifier plus safety rules) and treatment-to-admission flow.

Beds and admissions

Admissions, transfers and discharge, with a bed map per ward.

Laboratory

Separate result entry and verification, critical alerts, and result history drawn against reference ranges.

Imaging

Orders with a wrong-patient guard and radiologist-only reports.

Pharmacy

An expiry-first stock ledger, dispensing and returns. Drug-interaction and dose checks report “unavailable” until a drug database is connected.

Billing and insurance

Exact money arithmetic, tax rules held as data, idempotent payments, refunds and insurance claims.

Insights

Eleven operational metrics from a closed catalogue; there is no generated SQL.

Audit and setup

Audit viewer with chain verification and break-glass review; facilities, wards, formulary, tax rates and payers.

Also included

Around the modules.

Patient portal

A linked patient sees their own appointments, medicines, results and visit summaries, and can join a video visit.

FHIR R4 (read-only)

Patient, Encounter, Observation, AllergyIntolerance, MedicationRequest, Appointment and $everything.

Offline drafts

A note typed without a connection is kept encrypted on the device and syncs when the connection returns.

Draft-only AI

Off by default, never writes to the record, and always reviewed by a named person.

Not built yet: automated backups, critical-result paging, e-prescription, a payment gateway, patient self-booking, other languages and a native mobile app.
