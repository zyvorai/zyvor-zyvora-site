Southeast Asia

Hospitals in Southeast Asia can pilot this.

National ID formats, regulator registrations and exchange message shapes for the region's larger markets.

What fits

What is built for Southeast Asia.

National ID formats

Singapore NRIC and FIN, Malaysian MyKad and Thai national ID: format and check-digit validation, with a preferred name beside the legal name.

Exchange profiles

Message profiles for NEHR (Singapore) and SATUSEHAT (Indonesia), with completeness scoring and a local simulator. Working approximations, never sent anywhere.

Regulator registrations

Clinician licences and facility registrations by regulator body, such as MMC, with expiry.

Privacy

A request and breach register with due dates for the PDPA regime among others.

Tariffs and claims

Effective-dated payer rate cards, a self-pay price list, eligibility records with payer rules, and claim batch files in a payer's layout kept as data.

Public-health reporting

Notifiable diseases with deadline clocks and an anonymous line list.

Honest gaps

What is not there yet.

Specific to Southeast Asia. The general limits are on the intended-use page.

  • No live connection to NEHR, SATUSEHAT or any exchange.
  • No named payer specification and no live eligibility checks.
  • Thai, Malay, Indonesian and other languages are not built.
  • ID numbers are format-checked only, never looked up at a registry.
