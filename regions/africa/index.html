Africa

Hospitals and health networks in Africa can pilot this.

Programme registers, public-health reporting and mobile money, for facilities that report upwards and collect locally.

What fits

What is built for Africa.

HMIS-style reporting

Seven fixed indicators mapped to national identifiers, a monthly snapshot approved by a second administrator, then downloaded as a JSON or CSV file.

HIV, TB and malaria follow-up

Register templates, contact lists, and a list of patients who missed follow-up with logged attempts to reach them and a reasoned closure.

Immunisation

The facility's own schedule, due and overdue lists, doses given and wasted, and a printable child health card.

Notifiable diseases

A facility-authored list with immediate and weekly deadline clocks and an anonymous line list for the health authority.

Mobile money

Receipts from M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money and Orange Money by transaction code, matched against a statement file, with a daily till report.

Privacy

A request and breach register with due dates for several regimes, including POPIA and NDPA.

Runs on one server

One service and one database on a single host, with installer backup and restore commands.

Honest gaps

What is not there yet.

Specific to Africa. The general limits are on the intended-use page.

  • No live push to DHIS2 and no breakdown by age or sex yet.
  • Mobile money is record and match only: no gateway, nothing contacts a provider.
  • No automatic scheduled backups.
  • The interface is in English; French, Portuguese and Swahili are not built.
