Latin America

Hospitals in Latin America can pilot this.

Brazilian and Chilean ID formats and the pieces a pilot can try now. This is the region where we have the most still to build.

What fits

What is built for Latin America.

National ID formats

Brazilian CPF and Chilean RUT with check-digit validation, and a preferred name beside the legal name.

Regulator registrations

Clinician licences by regulator body, including Brazil's CRM, with expiry.

Tariffs and claim files

Effective-dated payer rate cards, a self-pay price list, and claim batch files with a problem report, kept as data.

Tax invoices

Tax IDs and a tax category per line, chained credit notes, and an unsigned export.

Records and privacy

Retention and legal holds, and a request and breach register that can use a custom set of days and hours.

The rest of ZYVORA

The full ward, theatre, laboratory, imaging and pharmacy modules, tried on invented patients.

Honest gaps

What is not there yet.

Specific to Latin America. The general limits are on the intended-use page.

  • Spanish and Portuguese are not built; only a draft Arabic and Hindi screen exists.
  • No named claim specification (for example TISS or RIPS) and no tax e-invoicing.
  • No national exchange profile for the region.
  • IDs are format-checked only, never looked up at a registry.

Is ZYVORA worth a pilot in Latin America?
