India

Hospitals in India can pilot this.

ZYVORA has the statutory and billing pieces Indian hospitals ask about, each with its limits stated.

What fits

What is built for India.

Government scheme billing

Public schemes with co-pay, caps and packages, enrolment with an authorisation, and an invoice split between scheme and patient.

Tax invoices

Tax IDs and a tax category on every line, credit notes chained to the invoice, and an unsigned export. Pharmacy supplier invoices carry a GST split.

Controlled drugs

Drug schedules H, H1, X and NDPS, prescriber registration numbers, numbered controlled prescriptions with validity, and a second-person inspector export.

Medico-legal cases

A case register with injury findings, police intimation, sealed samples and chain of custody.

Public-health reporting

A notifiable-disease register with deadline clocks, immunisation schedules with defaulter lists, and monthly HMIS-style report export.

Records and consent

ABDM-style local record sharing with consents and a log, birth and death registration worksheets, and a privacy register that includes the DPDP regime.

Hindi, in draft

A right-to-left-aware locale framework with draft Hindi and Arabic for one screen, marked pending review.

Honest gaps

What is not there yet.

Specific to India. The general limits are on the intended-use page.

  • No live connection to the ABDM gateway and no ABDM certification.
  • Aadhaar numbers are deliberately not stored.
  • Consent management beyond the local log is not built.
  • No tax-authority clearance or signing; the export is an unsigned file.
  • Hindi is a draft for one screen only.

Is ZYVORA worth a pilot in India?

Synthetic data only. Not a certified medical product. No price is an offer until we agree one together.
