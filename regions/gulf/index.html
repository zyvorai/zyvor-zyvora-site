Gulf states

Hospitals in the Gulf can pilot this.

Arabic layouts, the regional ID format and the national-exchange message shapes, as local simulators.

What fits

What is built for Gulf states.

Arabic and right-to-left

A locale framework that mirrors the layout, with draft Arabic for one screen, marked pending review.

Regional identifiers

Saudi Iqama and national ID format and check-digit validation, with a social (preferred) name beside the legal name.

National exchange profiles

Message profiles for Malaffi and NABIDH, with completeness scoring and a local simulator and rejection queue. Working approximations, never sent anywhere.

Regulator registrations

Clinician licences and facility registrations by regulator body, such as DHA and SCFHS, with expiry and an optional rule that blocks work without a licence.

Tax invoices and certificates

Tax IDs per line and chained credit notes with an unsigned export; numbered medical certificates with a public verification code.

Records governance

Retention rules, legal holds, two-person disposal certificates, and a disclosure ledger with multi-year accounting.

Honest gaps

What is not there yet.

Specific to Gulf states. The general limits are on the intended-use page.

  • No live exchange: the Malaffi and NABIDH profiles are approximations, not certified specifications.
  • No claims connection (for example NPHIES) and no tax e-invoicing clearance.
  • Only one screen has draft Arabic.
  • Regulator registration numbers are format-checked only, never verified with the regulator.
