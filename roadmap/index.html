Not built yet

Honest gaps.

These are the ones we get asked about most.

Sending prescriptions

Numbered prescription documents exist; transmission to a pharmacy or national system does not.

Real payment and messaging

Payment links and WhatsApp are labelled simulators; no gateway or account is connected.

National exchanges, live

Exchange and ABDM-style records are built as local records and simulators. No live connection and no certification.

Patient self-booking

Not built.

Scheduled backups

The installer has backup and restore commands; scheduled, monitored backups are for the operator to arrange.

Full translation

A first draft of Arabic and Hindi covers one screen. The rest of the interface is English.

A released mobile app

A first build exists and has never been run on a phone.

Imaging worklist service

A DICOM C-FIND worklist service and MPPS are not built.
